About Us

Aviora Crochet is a boutique Indian handmade gifting brand built around intentional, personalized, slow-crafted crochet products.

We believe gifts should carry time, care and human emotion, as an alternative to mass-produced, hurried gifting. Founded and run by Alok Kumar Dwivedi as a proprietorship, Aviora is in its development and growth phase, actively building its range, processes and partnerships.

How We Work

  • Retail orders: made on demand with premium handcrafting, via Instagram or our online store.
  • Mass orders: handled by call and email. We confirm requirements, pricing, timelines and quantity, then deliver through our production network.
  • Single point of contact: every deal is made directly through Aviora Crochet, so customers and partners always deal with one accountable team for production, quality and delivery.
Supplier policy. Our production partners work exclusively through Aviora Crochet. Direct contact between customers or partners and our suppliers is not permitted; all negotiations and orders go through us.
